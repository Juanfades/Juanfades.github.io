Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy describes how "agente-clips" (the Untold Side publishing tool, "the app") handles information. The app is operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Untold Side and is used only by that owner.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What information the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives an OAuth access token that lets it act on the owner's YouTube channel. The app uses it only to:

  • read the name and ID of the owner's own channel, to confirm the correct channel is connected;
  • upload the channel's own videos and set their title, description, tags, privacy status and scheduled publication time.

The app does not access, collect or store information about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube user, and it does not read private messages, watch history or analytics.

How information is stored and shared

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file. It is not sent to any server other than Google's, and it is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. The app does not use cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind.

How to revoke access and delete data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Revoking access makes the stored token useless; the owner can also delete the local token file. To request deletion of any information related to the app, write to the contact address below; requests are answered within 7 days.
