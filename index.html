Untold Side

The fact you didn't know about something you know.

Untold Side is a YouTube channel of short narrated videos about history, video games, pop culture, success stories, science and curiosities. Each Short tells one surprising, sourced fact in under a minute.

Watch the channel: youtube.com/@UntoldSideFacts

About the Untold Side publishing tool

"agente-clips" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Untold Side channel. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own videos, set their title, description and publication time. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account.
